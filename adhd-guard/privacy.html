ADHD Guard Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026
Developer: Kirill (GitHub: funkydevil)
Contact: kp.gentle.support@gmail.com

How the app works with your information

ADHD Guard evaluates your active app and, with your permission, the active tab URL in a supported browser. It uses this information on your Mac to apply your focus rules, show reminders and help you return to a previously focused app or website.

The app stores your focus-app list, distracting-site list, reminder settings, custom reminder phrases, reminder positions and a short recent activity list in its local settings. Recent activity can include app names, app identifiers, website domains and full page URLs, including their paths or query parameters. These values are not uploaded by ADHD Guard.

When you ask to return to a saved website, the app may inspect open tab URLs to find a matching tab. If no matching tab is found, it may ask your browser to reopen the saved URL. Visiting that page is then handled by your browser and the website.

Permissions

macOS Automation permission lets ADHD Guard communicate with supported browsers for URL checks and focus return. You can manage this permission in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.

When you choose an app for your focus list, ADHD Guard reads the selected application bundle's name and identifier.

For optional idle reminders, the app checks the time elapsed since keyboard and mouse events. It does not capture typed text. The app does not request microphone, screen recording, Accessibility or full-disk access.

Transmission and third-party services

ADHD Guard does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not transmit your app or browsing activity to the developer. No account is required.

Apple's App Store, macOS services, your browser and the websites you visit operate under their own privacy practices. Backups and device-management tools you use may include local app data independently of ADHD Guard.

Your controls and local storage

You can edit or remove individual focus rules and change your reminder settings in the app. Turning Focus mode off stops reminders, but recent activity continues to update locally while the app is running. Quit ADHD Guard to stop the app entirely.

Settings and recent activity persist across app restarts. The recent activity lists are limited in size, so newer entries replace older entries. Resetting settings is not a complete deletion of recent activity or all stored app data. To request instructions for removing the app's local stored data, contact kp.gentle.support@gmail.com. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your Mac.

Support by email

For support or privacy questions, email kp.gentle.support@gmail.com. The developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond to your request. Email is handled through Gmail. Please share only the information needed to explain the issue.

Support on GitHub

Support and privacy questions can be submitted through GitHub Issues. Please include ADHD Guard in the issue title. GitHub processes your account information and any content you submit under its Privacy Statement.

Issues are public. Do not post sensitive information, private browsing URLs or screenshots containing personal data. The developer uses the information you choose to share to respond to your request; opening an issue does not give the developer access to data stored on your Mac.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, when serving the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Privacy requests

For questions or requests about information you have shared by email or in an issue, contact kp.gentle.support@gmail.com. You can manage your issue content through GitHub, subject to GitHub's controls and policies. The developer cannot access information that remains only on your Mac.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date identifies the latest revision. Contact kp.gentle.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to the developer.
