Back on Task Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026
Developer: Kirill (GitHub: funkydevil)
Contact: kp.gentle.support@gmail.com

This policy covers Back on Task (formerly ADHD Guard), including usage analytics introduced in version 1.1. Earlier versions do not contain the PostHog integration.

How the app works with your information

Back on Task evaluates your active app and, with your permission, the active tab URL in a supported browser. It uses this information on your Mac to apply your focus rules, show reminders and help you return to a previously focused app or website.

The app stores your focus-app list, distracting-site list, reminder settings, custom reminder phrases, reminder positions and a short recent activity list in its local settings. Recent activity can include app names, app identifiers, website domains and full page URLs, including their paths or query parameters. These values are not uploaded by Back on Task.

When you ask to return to a saved website, the app may inspect open tab URLs to find a matching tab. If no matching tab is found, it may ask your browser to reopen the saved URL. Visiting that page is then handled by your browser and the website.

Permissions

macOS Automation permission lets Back on Task communicate with supported browsers for URL checks and focus return. You can manage this permission in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.

When you choose an app for your focus list, Back on Task reads the selected application bundle's name and identifier.

For optional idle reminders, the app checks the time elapsed since keyboard and mouse events. It does not capture typed text. The app does not request microphone, screen recording, Accessibility or full-disk access.

Transmission and third-party services

Back on Task does not include advertising and does not upload your browsing history, focus-app list, website rules or custom reminder phrases. No account is required to use the app.

Usage analytics is enabled by default. There is currently no in-app analytics switch. An existing stored preference to disable analytics is preserved when you upgrade. Contact the developer at kp.gentle.support@gmail.com with questions, deletion requests or requests for instructions to disable analytics. The developer cannot remotely change settings stored on your Mac. A link to this privacy policy is available in Settings.

While usage analytics is enabled, the app sends basic product events to PostHog Cloud in the European Union to help the developer understand usage and improve Back on Task. Events cover app launches, changes to Focus mode, rule counts and settings, reminder displays, reminder previews and return-button clicks. They include event times and identifiers, a random installation identifier, the app version and build, event-schema version, release environment, and the analytics SDK name and version. The installation identifier allows events from the same installation to be measured together; it is not your name, email address or advertising identifier.

Analytics does not include your Mac model, operating-system version, screen size, language, time zone, network connection type, the names or identifiers of the other apps you use, browser URLs or domains, page or window contents, your focus rules, custom reminder phrases, keystrokes or screen recordings. Session replay, automatic screen-view capture, automatic crash reporting and GeoIP enrichment are disabled. The app does not create a named PostHog person profile, use analytics for advertising or combine analytics with your browsing activity.

PostHog necessarily receives connection information, including an IP address, when it handles a network request. The analytics project is configured to discard IP addresses from stored analytics events, and the app disables GeoIP enrichment. Technical connection information may still be processed by PostHog's infrastructure to deliver and secure the service. This is separate from the local browser context used for focus reminders.

The current free PostHog plan provides one year of analytics-event history. This history window is not a promise that all stored data is automatically deleted at its end. The developer does not apply a separate automatic deletion schedule. You can request deletion of identifiable analytics records using the contact address above; the developer uses the deletion controls available through PostHog to handle such requests.

Quitting Back on Task stops new collection while the app is not running. Starting the app again resumes analytics if it remains enabled; quitting is not a persistent opt-out. Quitting or disabling analytics does not automatically delete events that PostHog has already received. For questions, access requests or deletion requests concerning analytics already sent, contact the developer using the address above. To locate the relevant records, the developer may need your installation identifier and can provide instructions for finding it in the app's local stored data. Your email address alone does not identify an app installation. The app does not send your support email address with analytics events.

PostHog processes analytics as a service provider. Its Privacy Policy describes its practices. Local analytics diagnostics are bounded to the most recent 200 product-event records and are cleared when analytics is turned off. Analytics is not needed for focus reminders to work.

Apple's App Store, macOS services, your browser and the websites you visit operate under their own privacy practices. Backups and device-management tools you use may include local app data independently of Back on Task.

Your controls and local storage

You can edit or remove individual focus rules and change your reminder settings in the app. Turning Focus mode off stops reminders, but recent activity continues to update locally while the app is running. Quit Back on Task to stop the app entirely.

Settings and recent activity persist across app restarts. The recent activity lists are limited in size, so newer entries replace older entries. Resetting settings is not a complete deletion of recent activity or all stored app data. To request instructions for removing the app's local stored data, contact kp.gentle.support@gmail.com. The developer cannot remotely access or delete data stored only on your Mac.

Support by email

For support or privacy questions, email kp.gentle.support@gmail.com. The developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond to your request. Email is handled through Gmail. Please share only the information needed to explain the issue.

Support on GitHub

Support and privacy questions can be submitted through GitHub Issues. Please include Back on Task in the issue title. GitHub processes your account information and any content you submit under its Privacy Statement.

Issues are public. Do not post sensitive information, private browsing URLs or screenshots containing personal data. The developer uses the information you choose to share to respond to your request; opening an issue does not give the developer access to data stored on your Mac.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, when serving the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Privacy requests

For questions or requests about analytics or information you have shared by email or in an issue, contact kp.gentle.support@gmail.com. You can manage your issue content through GitHub, subject to GitHub's controls and policies. The developer cannot access information that remains only on your Mac.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date identifies the latest revision. Contact kp.gentle.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to the developer.
